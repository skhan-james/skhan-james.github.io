Back Alley Arcade Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21
URL: https://skhan-james.github.io/alley-arcade/privacy-en.html

This policy explains how SUNGKU HAN ("we") handles information when you use the mobile game "Back Alley Arcade" (Korean title 골목 오락실, the "App") for Android and iOS (iPhone and iPad).

1. Summary

  • The App has no sign-up or user accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email, postal address, photos, contacts, or precise location.
  • Your game progress (coins, tickets, prizes, settings, records) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Ads, usage analytics, crash reporting, and remote configuration run on Google services (Google AdMob, Firebase). Leaderboards use Google Play Games Services on Android and Apple Game Center on iOS. These services receive device identifiers and app usage information as described below.
  • The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Ad privacy choices: Where consent applies, you can change your choices at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID (Android): You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path may differ by device). Deleting it reduces personalized ads.
  • Opting out of tracking (iOS): Choose "Ask App Not to Track" in the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, or later turn off tracking for this App in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Turning off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" on the same screen denies tracking requests from all apps.
  • Leaderboards (Android): If you do not sign in to Play Games, no scores are sent. You can delete game data from your Play Games profile (https://play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google Account.
  • Leaderboards (iOS): If you do not sign in to Game Center, no scores are sent. You can manage sign-in and your profile in iOS Settings → Game Center, and request a copy or deletion of data held by Apple at Apple's Data and Privacy page (https://privacy.apple.com).
  • On-device data: On Android, uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android settings deletes all game progress; on iOS, deleting the App does. Copies in device backups can be removed in your backup settings (Google account backup, iCloud Backup). We do not hold a copy and cannot restore it.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection: Because the App has no accounts, we usually cannot identify a specific person's data. You can still contact us at the address below and we will respond promptly within what we can verify. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK or certain US states), you may have additional rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

5. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea). If we learn that we have, we will take steps to delete it.

Note for parents: If you learn that your child under 14 has used the App, contact us at projecth7777@gmail.com. On-device records can be removed at once via Settings > Apps > Back Alley Arcade > Storage > Clear data on Android, or by deleting the App on iOS. On request we will explain how to delete identifiers and records held by the ad, analytics and leaderboard services.

6. Security

  • Communication between the App and Google and Apple services is encrypted (TLS/HTTPS).
  • The App does not communicate with any server other than Google and Apple services. The "Privacy policy" line in Settings simply opens this page in your browser.
  • The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo/file storage, or notification permissions. On Android, the only permissions it uses are normal permissions that need no separate approval screen, such as internet access and network state, and the advertising ID permissions added by the ads and analytics SDKs (see sections 2 and 4). On iOS, the only permission it asks for is App Tracking Transparency (ATT).
